# Shadows

Elevation in Ham-Masir is quiet and earned. Resting surfaces carry hierarchy
with border and surface color — shadow marks lift, selection, or float.
`none` is the resting grid tile; `card` is the resting card surface and the
hover lift on mentor tiles; `brand` is the primary/selected glow (filled
primary buttons, selected chips and options); `lifted` is floating chrome
(bottom sheet, toast, side menu, support button, suggestion dropdown).
`floating` is the theme-toggle hover lift; `dropdown` deepens the strip-card
hover. `popover`, `dialog` and `sheet` are defined and reserved — currently
unused. Values mirror `tokens.css`.
